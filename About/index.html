My journey in design started in the graphic design industry, where I focused on visual aesthetics and high-quality craft.

Driven by a curiosity for user behavior, I transitioned into product design. At Alibaba, I specialized in designing end-to-end experiences for complex B2B and B2C software products. Working in such a high-paced, large-scale environment taught me how to solve complex user problems and navigate strict business requirements without compromising on usability. I grew into a holistic product designer, carrying over my high standards for UI craft into every interface I build.

Today, I live and work in Berlin as a product designer. I continue to build impactful software products, always bringing the same dedication and passion for creating experiences that people love to use.
